network breaking | Login encryption and compression must now be explicitly enabled by the server with `login_finished` or `apply_options`, and the client acknowledges the change with `login_acknowledged` or `options_applied`. Packet settings take effect only after acknowledgement.

worldgen breaking | Changed ore and scattered ore feature placement. Blocks discarded due to air exposure are no longer passed to the next replacement rule.

worldgen density breaking | Renamed density function `interpolated` to `upscale`. It samples the input at a reduced resolution and has `x`, `y`, and `z` axis configurations with a `mode` and integer `cell_size` between `1` and `64`. Supported modes are `nearest_neighbor`, `interpolate_linear`, `interpolate_cosine`, `interpolate_cubic`, and `b_spline_cubic`.
